Not yet. The pain is real, but it comes from publishing shared libraries, not from having seven repos.

Do the smaller thing first. Put the three shared libraries into one repo with one CI config and release them together. That removes most of the two-day cycle, and it costs about a week of work.

On CI time, nobody has measured anything, so both sides are guessing. Six minutes per service is your baseline. Someone should measure what a monorepo build actually costs before the team argues about it again.

The bigger risk is ownership. Nobody there has run a monorepo, and you have no platform engineer. A monorepo moves the cost from publishing into build tooling, and that tooling needs an owner.

If the library consolidation lands and the pain is still there, the monorepo case becomes a real one. You will also have people who know the build system by then.

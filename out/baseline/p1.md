Short version: webhook deliveries that fail no longer get logged and dropped — they go into a retry queue that survives restarts.

**What's in it** (6 commits, +412/-87, 9 files):

- `src/queue/RetryQueue.ts` — new, 180 lines. The core: exponential backoff with jitter, capped at 5 attempts.
- `src/webhooks/dispatch.ts` — the actual behavior change. Failed sends now enqueue instead of logging and dropping.
- `src/queue/store/RedisStore.ts` — new. Redis persistence so pending retries survive a restart.
- `migrations/0043_retry_queue.sql` — new `retry_jobs` table.
- `config/queue.yaml` — `maxAttempts: 5`, `baseDelayMs: 1000`.
- 14 new tests in `src/queue/__tests__/RetryQueue.test.ts`.

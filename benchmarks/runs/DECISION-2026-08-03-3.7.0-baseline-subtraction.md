# Decision: 3.7.0 — keep only the rules a baseline comparison earned

**Decision: kept.**

## Deviations from the protocol, stated up front

This record documents a release-scale revision, not a single rule edit, so it deviates
from the protocol in three ways. Each is stated here rather than papered over.

- The edits were evaluated as one batch. The protocol asks for one edit at a time.
- The harness was `probes/` scored by `scripts/measure.py`, one run per arm, not
  `benchmarks/generate.mjs` at three runs per arm. A single run detects large effects
  only.
- The raw outputs are committed under `out/` as plain reply text. They are not in this
  protocol's transcript format and carry no frontmatter, so the model, date, and run
  number were not recorded at generation time. The model is therefore not named in this
  record; naming one now would be an invention, not a record.

## The observation

Version 3.6.0's body spent its largest block of text on lexical rules nobody had ever
seen the baseline break. The question for every rule became: does the model already do
this without being told?

## Before and after

The full rule-by-rule before/after is in the `[3.7.0]` entry of `CHANGELOG.md`, and the
method is in `docs/HONEST-NUMBERS.md`. In summary: the banned-phrase list, the
vocabulary swap table, "don't restate the question", and the Band 6 vocabulary rule
moved out of the body into `references/plain-english.md`. The 25-word rule moved to
position one. The narration check was restored, two merged checks were split, and check
8 now names the category of advice measured to disappear.

## Recall, both sides

Recall was checked by reading, against the findings each probe's context contained.
Two items distinguish the arms, and both are advice attached to a moment other than
now.

| finding | baseline recall | skill recall |
|---|---|---|
| make the migration re-runnable before production | carried | dropped |
| do the invasive fix as its own revertible change | carried | dropped |

The skill side loses both, in every version tried, including the shipped one. Evals 5
and 7 encode these two items and ship red. The sharpened check 8 was re-run against
both probes: it now produces a deferred-consequence warning where no version had one,
at about 50 words per reply, and still not the two specific items above.

## Reasoning

The subtraction is earned: four rule groups had zero baseline violations across eight
replies, twice-checked with conversational probes written to bait them. The additions
are earned the hard way: the recall failure is documented, measured, and shipped as two
red evals instead of being hidden by relaxed assertions. Body size went from 3,343 to
3,393 tokens, which is the honest cost.

## What this established, and what it did not

Established: which lexical rules the baseline already follows, the size and shape of
the reply change, and that the dropped-advice category survives a targeted check.
Not established: effect sizes beyond one run per arm, behaviour on any second model,
and recall on findings outside the two items tracked. The 25-word ceiling claim is
governed by the repository's own 36-run measurement, which supersedes this run's count.
